Reading the fine print, translated

"Must be authorized to work in the US." Good news. Your OPT EAD is work authorization. You qualify.

"No sponsorship now or in the future." You can work there on OPT, but they won't do H-1B later. Fine for a first job, plan your next move.

"US citizenship required" / "security clearance." Skip it. Clearances don't go to OPT holders.

"No OPT/CPT." Skip it. They said the quiet part out loud.

Questions, answered

Is this really free?

Yes. The community and the job lists are free. TechGPS is a staffing firm, we make our money placing experienced engineers, not charging students.

Do you apply for me?

No. You apply directly to the company. We find the jobs, filter them for OPT, and help you put together a strong application.

What if a company asks about sponsorship?

On OPT you don't need sponsorship, you have an EAD. Say exactly that: "I am authorized to work in the US through OPT, no sponsorship required at this time." For STEM OPT extension, the company needs to be enrolled in E-Verify, ask before you accept.

I'm on STEM OPT / my OPT starts later. Does this still help?

Yes. The same entry-level roles fit, and the work-auth notes flag which employers play the long game.

Who runs this?
