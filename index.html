Reading the fine print, translated

"Must be authorized to work in the US." Good news. Your OPT EAD is work authorization. You qualify.

"No sponsorship now or in the future." You can work there on OPT, but they will not do H-1B later. Fine for a first job, plan your next move.

"US citizenship required" or "security clearance." Skip it. Clearances do not go to OPT holders.

"No OPT/CPT." Skip it. They said the quiet part out loud.

Questions, answered

Is this really free?

Yes. The site and the community are free. We make our money placing experienced engineers, not charging students. If you want 1 on 1 help, DM us and we will talk about options.

Do you apply for me?

No. You apply directly to the company. We find the jobs, filter them for OPT, and help you put together a strong application.

What if a company asks about sponsorship?

On OPT you do not need sponsorship, you have an EAD. Say exactly that: "I am authorized to work in the US through OPT, no sponsorship required at this time." For STEM OPT extension, the company needs to be enrolled in E-Verify, ask before you accept.

I'm on STEM OPT or my OPT starts later. Does this still help?

Yes. The same entry-level roles fit, and the work-auth notes flag which employers play the long game.
